Calendar-year mode always starts January 1. Fiscal years start on the first day of the chosen month.

Determines when the reporting year rolls over at local midnight. Default: UTC. Completion dates are never converted or changed.

What happens at rollover?

Completed work from earlier years appears in History. Unfinished and undated notes remain in Accomplishments. Future-dated completed work is available in All years.

Changing these settings regroups existing records immediately. Nothing is moved, archived, or deleted. History remains editable and available for reports. Archive and Trash keep their separate behavior.
