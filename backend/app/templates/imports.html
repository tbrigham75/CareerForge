Import source document

CareerForge reads source files only. It records path, filename, hash, time, and status; a completed source hash is skipped on re-import to prevent duplicate accomplishments.

Older Word .doc files must be saved as .docx before importing. Labeled Action, Metric, and Impact sections and table columns fill their matching fields. Missing fields stay blank. Entries remain raw notes for review. Re-importing a document from the older Word importer replaces untouched fragments with structured entries; replaced fragments remain recoverable in Trash. Edited entries are preserved.
